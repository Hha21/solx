- The leader is not uniformly ahead. It trails us on the top rows (we hold S 0.77-0.80 at 32,128, 64,96 and 8,320) and leads by about 0.02-0.03 S on the six wide-C rows (16,384 8,384 16,768 8,768 32,768 2,768). - The leader uses a multi-kernel pipeline linked by programmatic dependent launch. That is allowed by the harness docs but rejected by our lint. Each phase (conv + stats, GEMM1 + GELU + sumsq, GRN + GEMM2) is a separate kernel, so each phase can use its best tile shape and cta_group mode: 1SM GEMM1, pair GEMM2, which ptxas forbids mixing in one kernel. Prologues (weight conversion, plane copies, TMEM alloc) overlap the previous kernel's tail, so the launch gaps that cost us 1-1.5 us per launch on the portal disappear. (fits: Our H21 data: a launch costs 1-1.5 us more on the portal than on the bench, which is why single cooperative launches won. ptxas will not mix cta_group::1 and ::2. Pairs pay only where a CTA runs many stages. With PDL, the per-phase engine choice would be free.; test: Rented probe only, never shipped: chain D's phase A, GEMM1 and GEMM2 as 3 kernels with griddepcontrol at 8,768 and 16,384 and time them against tc_kernel. If PDL alone recovers >= 10 % on those rows, take the result to the operator to decide whether the PDL constraint should be re-cleared with NVIDIA. We do not use it in the meantime.)
