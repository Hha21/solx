### Notes
Arithmetic that frames every idea: one fp16 MMA pass of one GEMM costs 0.072 µs per 1000 tokens at 1.82 PF/s. FP16x3 on both GEMMs once is 6 passes (0.43 µs/1k); recomputing GEMM1 is 9 passes (0.65 µs/1k). The leader's implied times fit one linear law, about 10 µs fixed plus 0.69 µs/1k at every size, so the leader is a 6-pass design at roughly 60% MMA utilisation, not a recompute design. The exact-erf GELU is a second-order term nobody has priced: 2 MUFU per element is 0.29 µs/1k (58 µs at 64,56,56), on the critical path unless it overlaps the MMA. Per SM, a 128-token tile is 8.2 µs of MMA, 5.5 µs of MUFU, and 512 KB of fp16 hi/lo weight images (2.7 µs just to land in smem). Precision is fixed at FP16x3 (or 3xTF32) with fp32 accumulation; every candidate must log max error per workload over 10 rounds against the harness reference, margin is only ~20% of atol.
