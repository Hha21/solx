### Notes
The binding term at L is CUDA-core issue, not the MMA. At 64,56,56 there are 102.8M GELU elements. At about 40 issue slots per element (erff alone is about 29) that is about 217k clk per SM, or 145 µs at 1.5 GHz. The FP16x3 MMA for both GEMMs needs 87 µs.

The e4 round runs about 29.7 µs on the portal: 326 µs over 11 rounds. Against that, the MMA needs 8.2 µs and the epilogue about 13.7 µs, and they run mostly one after the other.

The second structural term is weight ingest. Token-stationary tiles pull 512 KB of hi/lo weights per CTA tile, about 4 KB per token. Tiles smaller than 128 tokens become ingest-bound at about 120 GB/s per SM. Hidden-sliced designs avoid this.

Precision dead end, by arithmetic: FP8 cross terms fail by about 5×, so 3 FP16 passes are the MMA floor. Ozaki/int8 is the same cost.

From predict_score: −10/−20/−45% on S/M/L gives 0.797, and −25/−60/−60% gives 0.868.
