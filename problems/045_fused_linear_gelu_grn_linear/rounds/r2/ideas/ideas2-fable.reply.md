### Notes
Two hardware terms dominate everything measured so far and neither is in the ledger's hypotheses: (1) the tcgen05 operand path is bound by the ~123 B/clk smem port at N<=128 (from #129 K), so a 128x128x16 FP16 MMA costs 66 clk against 64 ideal, 50 clk at N=64 against 32; (2) every CTA re-streams the same 512 KB weight image per 128-token tile, so 148 SMs hit the same 256 L2 lines in lock-step (plausibly 0.4-0.8 us per 32 KB unit of slice serialisation), which matches the 19 us fixed chain at S/M and ~24 us rounds at L where MMA is 8.2 us. TMEM holds exactly one 128-token fp32 h, which forbids overlap in the token-stationary family. Ideas below attack: the weight path (replicas, multicast), overlap (half-tiles, recompute), split-K reduction cost (bulk reduce, DSMEM), and three long shots. L is worth +0.0101 per 10%, M +0.0079, S +0.0053; the L/M-heavy scenario (S-12/M-40/L-50) models 0.8235.
